# Tailored CV and cover letter from facts only

## The one rule

Every claim about the owner — name, contacts, employers, dates, titles, skills, numbers, degrees, languages,
citizenship, availability — must be found in `facts.md`. If it is not there, it does not exist. You may
rephrase, select, order and emphasise; you may not add, round up, merge or infer. When the posting asks for
something the facts do not show, write `[MISSING: <what>]` and report it; do not paper over the gap.

## CV (markdown, one to two pages)

1. Header: name and contacts exactly as in facts.
2. Summary: 2–3 lines connecting the owner's real experience to this role, in the posting's vocabulary where
   the facts support it.
3. Skills: the facts' skills, the ones the posting names first. Do not list a skill the facts lack.
4. Experience: reverse chronological. Per role 2–4 bullets chosen for relevance to this posting; keep numbers
   exactly as given in facts.
5. Education, languages, other — only if in facts.

## Cover letter (under 250 words)

- Addressed to the company (a person only if the posting names one).
- First paragraph: the role and one concrete reason this company — from the posting text, not invented.
- Middle: two or three matches between the posting's needs and facts, each with a fact as proof.
- Close: availability and contact from facts. Plain, confident, no clichés ("passionate", "rockstar").

## Before saving, check

- Read every sentence and point to the line in facts.md it rests on. Delete what has no line.
- English throughout; dates and names spelled as in facts.
